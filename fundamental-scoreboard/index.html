Data-release candle order blocks

Log the 1-hour candle of the data release as the order block (its high/low). While price holds above it, it's an OB (support). Once price breaks and closes below its low, it becomes an inverted OB (iOB) — expected resistance on retest. Bias comes from the scoreboard.
