Release zones

Mark the 1-hour candle of each data release (rate decision, CPI, GDP, jobs…) and extend its high–low range into the future as a zone. Price above a zone → it acts as support; price below → resistance. A zone is a potential entry only when the scoreboard bias for that pair agrees: buy at support when the bias is bullish, sell at resistance when it is bearish.
