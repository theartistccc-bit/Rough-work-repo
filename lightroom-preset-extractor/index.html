Preset Extractor

Load your Presetpro zip downloads. Only the preset files (.dng, .xmp, .lrtemplate) are kept. PDFs, .DS_Store and __MACOSX junk are dropped.

    How to get them into Lightroom mobile
    1. Send to Lightroom opens the share sheet. Pick Lightroom (or "Save to Files" / "Save images").
    2. DNG presets arrive as photos. Open one, tap … → Create Preset, name it, save.
    3. XMP presets: in an edit, open Presets → … → Import Presets and choose the files or the zip.
