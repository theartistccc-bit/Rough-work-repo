What's really in your pot?

Across Nigeria, much of what's on the shelf isn't food anymore. It's dye, chemicals and shortcuts dressed up to look like food. Here's what Bushman exists to fight.

0

% of palm oil is fake

It's coloured with Sudan dye, blended with cheap oils and cut with chemicals. It looks red and tastes like nothing.

☠

Beans preserved with poison

Carbide, sniper and rat poison are used to keep weevils away in storage. You can't wash it off.

⚠

Fake drinks everywhere

Refilled bottles, counterfeit labels and unknown liquids. They're in shops, bars and parties.

🍯

“Honey” that's sugar syrup

Boiled sugar, glucose and colouring sold as raw honey from the forest.

How we keep it real

The Bush Quality path.

From the farm to your door, every step is checked. No shortcuts and no chemicals.

1

Known farmers, known land

We buy straight from farmers and cooperatives we've visited ourselves. Every batch is tied to a farm, a village and a harvest date.

2

Clean processing

Palm oil is pressed traditionally from fresh fruit bunches with no dye and no blending. Rice is destoned and parboiled with clean water. Honey is cold-strained, never boiled or cut.

3

Chemical-free storage

Beans and grains are stored in hermetic bags with no carbide, no sniper and no rat poison. We use airtight storage and natural pest control.

4

Tested before it ships

Batches are sampled and checked for adulterants, dyes and residues. Anything that fails goes back.

5

Sealed to your door

Tamper-evident packaging with a batch code. Scan it to see where your food came from.

The Bush Quality Seal

When you see this seal, the product has passed our five-step path. If any Bushman product isn't what we say it is, we'll refund you in full and pull the whole batch.
