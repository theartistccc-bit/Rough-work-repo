Did you beat the closing line?

After kick-off, enter the odds you took and Pinnacle's closing odds for all outcomes. Positive CLV over 100+ bets is the clearest sign of a real edge, well before profit shows.

Example: draw taken at 4.50
HomeDrawAway Pinnacle close

  • CLV = odds you took ÷ fair closing odds − 1.
  • Keep a list of every bet with its CLV. Average it after each 50 bets.
  • Average CLV above +2% means the method is working, even through losing runs.
